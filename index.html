Before you begin: privacy and important information We do not ask for your name or contact details. Cloudflare processes your answers to calculate results. Your answers are not shown publicly or to other participants. If you choose to save them, the project team can view them in a password-protected dashboard for academic analysis; saved responses are kept until deleted. Cloudflare provides the website, database, and optional AI service. This questionnaire is not a diagnosis, and its wording and scoring have not been clinically validated.

Question 25 uses a different scale: 0 = not at all difficult, 1 = somewhat, 2 = very, and 3 = extremely difficult.

Questions 1–21 refer to the last 2 weeks; questions 22–24 refer to the past few months. You can leave either consent option unchecked and still receive your results.
