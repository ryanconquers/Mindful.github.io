Before you begin: privacy and important information You take the check-in while signed in to a free patient account (your name and email). Cloudflare processes your answers to calculate results, and each completed check-in is saved to your account. It is visible only to you and to the doctor you link to your account, and is never shown publicly or to other participants. The project team may view saved answers without your name or email in a password-protected dashboard for academic analysis. You can unlink your doctor or delete your account and records at any time. Cloudflare provides the website and database; Google provides the optional AI reflection service. This questionnaire is not a diagnosis, and its wording and scoring have not been clinically validated.

Question 25 uses a different scale: 0 = not at all difficult, 1 = somewhat, 2 = very, and 3 = extremely difficult.
